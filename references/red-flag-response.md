# Red-Flag Response Boundary

Use this card only when the described facts may require urgent medical or safety action.

## Response Order

1. State the immediate action and urgency plainly.
2. Name only the observed facts that make escalation necessary.
3. Repeat the already known facts the caregiver should communicate to emergency services or the receiving clinician.
4. Stop. Follow emergency-dispatch, poison-control, clinician, or current official instructions.

Do not delay urgent action to browse, complete a profile, rank behavioral causes, or discuss a parenting method.

## Do Not Add From Memory

Unless a current responsible authority or clinician instruction supports it for this exact situation, do not add:

- a diagnostic label or imaging threshold;
- universal instructions to keep the child awake;
- universal fasting, fluid, milk, food, or medicine restrictions;
- a maneuver, home treatment, or monitoring interval;
- a claim that one absent sign makes home observation safe.

When verification is unavailable, keep the response at urgent escalation plus the user's stated facts. Do not turn uncertainty into invented first aid.

## Useful Handoff Facts

Use only facts the caregiver already supplied, such as the event, observed symptoms, responsiveness, breathing, known exposure, relevant medicine or product identity. Do not ask extra questions before urgent escalation.
