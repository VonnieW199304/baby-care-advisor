# Source Routing

Read this file only when the answer depends on a current, medical, safety-critical, or externally verifiable fact. Do not browse every ordinary behavior question.

This is an open routing map, not a whitelist. A source that is not named here may still be useful after its identity, remit, evidence, scope, date, and conflicts are checked.

## Non-Negotiable Retrieval Rules

1. Write the exact fact to verify before searching.
2. Choose the source that governs that fact. A source authoritative for vaccination is not automatically authoritative for sleep, development, or feeding.
3. Match country or region, child's age, care setting, document version, publication date, and effective date.
4. Open the original page or document. Search snippets, reposts, summaries, and AI citations are discovery aids only.
5. If the source cannot be accessed or does not answer the exact question, write `未完成核验` or `该来源不能确认这一点`. Never turn a failed search into `没有风险` or `没有规定`.

## Source Layers

Use layers by claim jurisdiction, not as a universal trust ranking:

- **Clinical, public-health, safety, and regulatory sources**: current local authorities; WHO; professional pediatric policy such as AAP or Canadian Paediatric Society; NICE; product regulators such as CPSC. Use for the exact health, safety, guideline, or regulatory claim within their population and market.
- **Evidence translation and caregiving implementation**: WHO/UNICEF programmes, AAP HealthyChildren, NHS family guidance, and Raising Children Network. Use to translate evidence into household actions; do not use them to replace diagnosis, formal policy, or an exact product record.
- **Parenting and early-education method originals**: AMI Montessori, RIE/Pikler, Attachment Parenting International, or an author's/publisher's original method description. Use to establish what a method teaches; independently verify its health, safety, and causal claims.
- **Professional experience, community reports, and commercial material**: use for feasibility, recurring difficulties, product claims, and user experience. They do not independently establish medical safety, developmental diagnosis, or long-term benefit.

| Responsive care and early learning | [WHO nurturing care](https://www.who.int/teams/maternal-newborn-child-adolescent-health-and-ageing/child-health/nurturing-care); [WHO early childhood development guideline](https://www.who.int/publications/i/item/97892400020986/); [UNICEF Care for Child Development](https://www.unicef.org/documents/care-child-development) | Broad evidence-informed principles and practical coaching for responsive caregiving, play, communication, and early learning | Population programmes do not prove that one branded activity accelerates intelligence or treats delay |
| Evidence-translated everyday parenting | [Raising Children Network](https://raisingchildren.net.au/about-us/what-we-do), AAP HealthyChildren, UNICEF parenting materials | Plain-language household practices and options | Check the underlying source for high-stakes or disputed claims; advice may reflect another country's services and culture |
| Vaccination in China | [NHC National Immunization Programme Schedule, 2021](https://www.nhc.gov.cn/wjw/c100175/202103/951e284920bd4a908ba10875694f0f0e.shtml), then current local CDC or vaccination-clinic notice | National schedule and locally applicable implementation details | Verify current year, region, vaccine type, dose history, contraindication, and catch-up context |
| Medicines and medical devices in China | [NMPA public query portal](https://www.nmpa.gov.cn/zwfwqjd/index.html?type=pc), approved label or instructions, then clinician or pharmacist | Product identity, approved indication, contraindications, warnings, and instructions | Registration is not a personalized prescription. Never derive a dose from age alone or silently substitute concentration/formulation |
| Baby product recall or exact-product safety | Route the product decision to the purchase skill; use SAMR recall/quality sources and exact manufacturer notice | Exact market, model, batch, and recall action | A missing recall record does not prove safety; foreign-market notices require exact market/model matching |

## Retrieval Sequence

1. Extract: country or region, age or corrected age, exact concern, relevant date, and any product/medicine identity.
2. Search the responsible authority's official domain using the exact topic, document title, standard number, medicine name, or model.
3. Open the source and check scope, version, date, and whether it is still current.
4. Quote or paraphrase only the part that changes the action.
5. Stop after the decision is sufficiently supported. Usually one to three material sources are enough.

## Source Conflicts

- Prefer the currently applicable local authority for schedules, services, and legal requirements.
- Use international guidance for broad evidence-based principles, while stating when local implementation may differ.
- When two credible sources differ, check population, age, setting, outcome, date, and recommendation strength before calling them contradictory.
- Preserve uncertainty when evidence is mixed. Do not manufacture a single universal rule.

## Source Not In This Map

1. Identify who published it and whether the page is original, licensed, quoted, or reposted.
2. State the publisher's remit: regulator, clinical body, research group, method owner, practitioner, seller, community, or individual.
3. Split the supplied content into safety/medical claims, testable factual claims, practical strategies, and values or preferences.
4. Verify each material claim with the source layer responsible for it. Do not reject a useful practice merely because its origin is non-clinical, and do not promote it to clinical evidence because it sounds professional.
5. Check age, country/market, setting, date, evidence cited, conflicts of interest, and whether the recommendation is feasible for this family.
6. Give a bounded action and say what would change it. Add a new permanent source or method card only after repeated use or clear general value.
